# Urbanic

> Urbanic is an online fast-fashion retailer selling women's apparel and accessories through app-first, localized storefronts, including in.urbanic.com (India) and mx.urbanic.com (Mexico). It is a consumer ecommerce business and does not publish a public developer API, developer portal, or API documentation.

Urbanic is part of the API Evangelist network as a company profile. Its storefront frontend is driven by a first-party consumer API (api-shop-in.urbanic.com) that is not offered to third-party developers. No OpenAPI, SDKs, webhooks, or /.well-known/ discovery documents were found on its public surface.
